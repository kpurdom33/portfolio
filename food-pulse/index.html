Context makes the chart

Interest, with the right perspective.

Use these signals to explore regional differences and spot questions worth investigating.

01

Relative, not absolute

Google normalizes sampled search data to the location and time window. An index of 100 marks peak relative interest in that chart. It isn't a search count, and separate charts have their own scales.

02

Your terms shape the story

A state's leading term wins only among the selected foods. Queries can reflect recipes, restaurants, news, or curiosity. They don't establish what residents eat most.

03

Refreshing responsibly

The dashboard requests the latest chart every 15 minutes while this tab is visible and online. Google controls the underlying data cadence. Small regions may have insufficient search volume.
