Selected work

Business strategy, analytics, and application development.

Food Logger

1. The problem

Food names, nutrition records, and serving sizes don't follow one format. A useful log needs to turn those inconsistent inputs into comparable amounts.

2. The solution

I built a Python app that cleans USDA nutrition data, matches food entries, and calculates calories and protein for the selected portion.

3. Making it reliable

Uncertain matches ask for confirmation. Unsupported units block saving. Automated tests cover portion calculations, manual entries, and separate visitor logs.

Connecting business strategy with implementation planning and decision support.

Coordination built into the plan
